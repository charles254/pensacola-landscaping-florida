Interlocking Concrete Paver Patterns & Edge Restraint Mechanics

Interlocking herringbone and running bond patterns distribute vehicular and foot traffic loads evenly across the surface, while heavy-duty spiked edge restraints prevent perimeter shifting.

  • Laying 45-degree or 90-degree herringbone configurations for maximum interlock strength
