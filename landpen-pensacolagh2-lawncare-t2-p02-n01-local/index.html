Value Engineering Landscape Designs Without Sacrificing Visual Aesthetics

Value engineering replaces delicate high-cost exotics with resilient local cultivars, optimizes irrigation zones to reduce valve counts, and prioritizes long-lived perennial groundcovers.

  • Installing 3-gallon nursery stock rather than expensive mature 15-gallon specimens
  • Combining automated drip irrigation lines into single multi-outlet manifolds
  • Using localized gravel crushed stone pathways in low-traffic zones instead of pavers
  • Selecting longleaf pine straw mulch as a renewable, low-cost organic ground cover
